# Hackathon alignment update

Source: the full participant guide pasted by the project owner on 30 October 2026.
This supersedes conflicting statements in the earlier build-plan copy; it is not
an independent account/credit verification.

- Paper2Proof targets **Push the Frontier** through experimental verification.
- Meaningful runtime use of Meta models is required. The offline provider template,
  local reviewed solver and coding-assistant use do not satisfy that requirement.
- The guide does not say every component must be an agent. Model-based extraction,
  planning, implementation and bounded repair should be paired with deterministic
  policy enforcement, isolated execution and independent evaluation.
- The guide explicitly requires a **three-minute demo video**. The old plan's claim
  that three minutes is only our recommendation is superseded.
- Repository visibility, accessible demo/video links, team attribution and event-built
  contributions must be checked before submission. The contribution log records actual work.
- The pasted judging rubric and prize tables contain placeholders; no scoring weights
  have been inferred.
- Account credits remain the owner's responsibility. No credit, billing or live API
  action is authorized merely by storing keys in `.env`.

The intended demonstration is one genuinely sourced, feasible claim through Meta
generation and isolated execution, plus an honest negative/missing-information case.
The current engineering document and offline preview are clearly labeled development
fixtures. They must not be presented as Meta inference or a reproduced research paper.
